KNOW WHAT TO LOOK FOR

Small link details can matter

These are useful clues to check before you trust a link.

01

Read the real domain

Check the name immediately before the final suffix. Extra words or misspellings can make a lookalike site seem familiar.

02

Be careful with shortened links

A shortened address hides where it leads. If you weren't expecting it, verify with the sender through a separate trusted channel.

03

HTTPS is not proof

The lock icon only means a connection is encrypted. It does not confirm that the site or the person asking is legitimate.

!
When in doubt, go directly to the source

Open the official app or type its known address yourself. Never enter a password, payment details, or one-time code from a link in an unexpected message.

A low score cannot guarantee that a link is safe. This check looks at URL structure and does not verify the website's current content or reputation.

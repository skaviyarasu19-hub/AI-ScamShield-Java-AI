Common pressure tactics to notice

Scam messages often try to make careful thinking feel difficult or urgent.

01

Urgency or threats

“Act now” or threats to close an account can be used to pressure you into skipping verification.

02

Requests for secrets

Never share passwords, PINs, or one-time codes with someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

03

Unexpected money or prizes

Be cautious of surprise winnings, guaranteed returns, job offers, or requests to pay a fee upfront.

✓
Pause, verify, then decide

Contact the organisation using a number or website you already trust—not the link or contact details in the message. Report and block suspicious senders when appropriate.

This analysis looks for common patterns and may miss new tactics or flag unusual but legitimate messages. Treat its result as a guide, not a guarantee.
